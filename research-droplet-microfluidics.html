Research area 01

Droplet

Turning tiny droplets into high-throughput reactors for single-cell biology, molecular diagnostics, material synthesis, and 3D culture.

Why it matters

Thousands of experiments. One compact platform.

Droplets isolate samples into precisely controlled miniature compartments, enabling fast, scalable, and quantitative experimentation.

We engineer systems for droplet generation, manipulation, detection, and downstream analysis. By controlling volume, composition, and flow with high precision, these platforms can reduce sample consumption while increasing throughput across biological and biomedical applications.

Current themes

What we investigate

01

Single-cell analysis

Compartmentalising, cultivating, screening, and measuring individual cells at high throughput.

02

Digital diagnostics

Combining droplets with molecular assays for sensitive detection and absolute quantification.

03

3D cell models

Generating uniform tumour spheroids for drug screening and nanomedicine assessment.
