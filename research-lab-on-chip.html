Research area 02

Lab on Chip

Integrating fluid handling, cell analysis, and biological assays into compact chips for biomedical research and screening.

Why it matters

Complex biology. Precise control.

Microfluidic chips provide controlled environments for studying cells, delivering chemical gradients, and measuring biological responses with small sample volumes.

We design and fabricate integrated microfluidic devices that combine sample preparation, cell handling, culture, and analysis. These platforms support reproducible experiments, high-throughput imaging, and drug screening in physiologically relevant models.

Current themes

What we investigate

01

Integrated microdevices

Combining microfabrication, fluid control, and assay integration within compact chips.

02

Cell analysis

Controlling, separating, and imaging cells for high-throughput biological measurements.

03

Culture & drug screening

Using microwells and concentration gradients to study 3D cell models and treatment responses.
