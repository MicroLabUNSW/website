Lab on Chip

We integrate cell handling, culture, and analysis into microfluidic chips for reproducible biomedical experiments and high-throughput drug screening.

01

Integrated microdevices

Fragmented lab workflows consume samples and limit reproducibility. We integrate fluid control and assays on compact chips for efficient biomedical experiments.

02

Cell analysis

Cell diversity demands rapid, precise measurements. We develop microfluidic separation and imaging platforms to examine large cell populations at high throughput.

03

Culture & drug screening

Reliable drug screening requires controlled cell environments. We combine microwell culture and drug gradients on chips to compare treatment responses reproducibly.
