Principal Investigator

Associate Professor Ming Li

NHMRC Emerging Leadership Fellow and Associate Professor at UNSW Sydney.

Ming leads research in droplet microfluidics, biosensors, and lab-on-chip technologies. She received her PhD from the University of Wollongong and held postdoctoral appointments at the University of Houston and UCLA before joining Macquarie University and later UNSW Sydney.
